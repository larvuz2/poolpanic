A. The fantasy (258 characters)
You are Coach Waters, the only lifeguard at 14 ridiculous pools. Sort swimmers into lanes, chase the dog off the deck, talk Karen down and fish out what nobody wants to name, all to fill the Date Fund for Sunday with Marina. Solo or online co-op for up to 3.

B. The incident list (259 characters)
A comedy pool-management game where everything goes wrong at once: cannonballs, cramps, a dog on the deck, a power cut, Karen. Keep 14 pools afloat for six shifts a week and save up for Sunday's date with Marina. No two shifts alike. Online co-op for up to 3.

C. The deadpan one-liner (254 characters)
It's a lovely day at the pool, and you are the only lifeguard. Run 14 chaotic venues, from a rolling cruise ship to a hot spring full of snow monkeys, survive the week and make it to your Sunday date with Marina. Solo or online co-op for up to 3 coaches.
